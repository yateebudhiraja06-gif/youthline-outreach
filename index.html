Guide

YouthLine listens, advocates, and builds.

Listening. We listen to our peers ages 24 and under, supporting them through problems no matter how small or big. Who knows youth better than their peers? That is why our volunteers are from 15 to 25 years old. They have over 65 hours of training with certifications in Youth Mental Health First Aid and safeTALK, and also get opportunities throughout the year to further their education.

Advocating. We advocate by using the trends that we see on the lines, like concerns over AI, to testify and inform legislation that helps youth in the state of Oregon. Our volunteers witnessed Governor Tina Kotek sign SB 1546 into law, a bill that requires AI platforms to connect at-risk youth to crisis resources. A volunteer testified in support of it. YouthLine representatives created an advisory council to work with Senator Ron Wyden to further educate the federal government on how to help youth.

Building. We build our reach by having 155 active youth volunteers who answer from 4 to 10pm, but we want to expand our times and services. Outside of those current hours, adults are available by phone. We have established two locations in Hawai’i with 20 active volunteers. Not only are we expanding, we give our volunteers the opportunity to practice industry standards such as HIPAA and electronic notes.
